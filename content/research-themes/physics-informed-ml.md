Physics-informed machine learning integrates governing equations, mechanics, numerical methods, and data-driven models to develop interpretable and reliable computational tools for engineering systems.

This theme is suitable for students interested in scientific machine learning, structural dynamics, PDEs, inverse problems, and mechanics-guided artificial intelligence.

---

## Project Directions

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">

### Physics-Informed Learning for Structural Dynamics

Develop machine learning models constrained by equations of motion for predicting and identifying structural dynamic response.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** structural dynamics, neural networks, PINNs, numerical integration, Python  
**Expected outcomes:** physics-guided prediction models and publishable computational studies

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### PDE Discovery for Engineering Systems

Develop sparse and interpretable methods to discover governing equations from noisy or incomplete data.

**Suitable for:** PhD  
**Methods:** sparse regression, PDE discovery, system identification, scientific computing  
**Expected outcomes:** interpretable models for nonlinear dynamic systems and scientific discovery

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Mechanics-Guided Neural Networks

Develop neural network models that respect physical constraints such as equilibrium, compatibility, boundary conditions, and conservation laws.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** continuum mechanics, neural networks, constrained optimization, numerical methods  
**Expected outcomes:** interpretable ML models for engineering applications

</div>

</div>

## Useful Background

- Structural dynamics
- Applied mechanics
- Numerical methods

- Differential equations

Students do not need to know all these areas before starting. A strong interest in both mechanics and computation is more important.
