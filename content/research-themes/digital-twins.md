Digital twins for civil infrastructure combine mechanics-based models, sensing data, state estimation, and machine learning to enable real-time monitoring, prediction, and decision support.

This theme is suitable for students interested in structural dynamics, finite element modelling, sensing, system identification, uncertainty quantification, and AI-assisted infrastructure management.

---

## Project Directions

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">

### Digital Twin of an RC Bridge or Building Frame

Develop a computational digital twin that combines finite element modelling, vibration data, and state estimation for monitoring structural condition.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** FEM, structural dynamics, Kalman filtering, Bayesian updating, sensor fusion, Python/MATLAB  
**Expected outcomes:** digital twin workflow, simulation studies, validation using experimental or field data

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Physics-Guided Model Updating

Develop model-updating methods that combine sensor data with physics-based structural models for improved prediction of structural response.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** inverse problems, optimization, uncertainty quantification, structural dynamics  
**Expected outcomes:** robust model-updating framework and publishable computational results

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Uncertainty-Aware Infrastructure Decision Support

Develop uncertainty-aware digital twin methods for maintenance planning and condition-based decision-making.

**Suitable for:** PhD  
**Methods:** Bayesian inference, reliability analysis, digital twins, decision support  
**Expected outcomes:** framework for risk-informed infrastructure monitoring

</div>

</div>

## Useful Background

- Structural dynamics
- Finite element modelling
- Signal processing
- Python/MATLAB programming

- Uncertainty quantification

Prior expertise in all areas is not required. Students can enter from structural engineering, mechanics, computation, or AI/ML backgrounds.
