Uncertainty quantification focuses on understanding, modelling, and propagating uncertainty in sensing, modelling, prediction, and decision-making for civil infrastructure systems.

This theme is suitable for students interested in reliability, Bayesian inference, probabilistic modelling, digital twins, structural health monitoring, and risk-informed infrastructure decisions.

---

## Project Directions

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">

### Uncertainty-Aware Structural Health Monitoring

Develop methods to quantify uncertainty in damage detection, localization, and condition assessment of infrastructure systems.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** Bayesian inference, probabilistic modelling, SHM, signal processing, machine learning  
**Expected outcomes:** uncertainty-aware SHM frameworks and more reliable damage assessment workflows

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Bayesian Model Updating for Digital Twins

Develop probabilistic model-updating methods that combine finite element models, sensor data, and prior knowledge for digital twin applications.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** Bayesian updating, FEM, structural dynamics, optimization, sensor data assimilation  
**Expected outcomes:** robust model-updating tools for infrastructure monitoring and prediction

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Risk-Informed Infrastructure Decision Support

Develop computational frameworks that connect monitoring data, model uncertainty, and decision-making for maintenance and management of civil infrastructure.

**Suitable for:** PhD  
**Methods:** reliability analysis, Bayesian decision theory, digital twins, uncertainty propagation  
**Expected outcomes:** decision-support frameworks for safer and more resilient infrastructure systems

</div>

</div>
