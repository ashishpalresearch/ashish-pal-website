Drone-based monitoring uses unmanned aerial platforms, cameras, and non-contact sensing methods for inspection, measurement, and monitoring of civil infrastructure systems.

This theme is suitable for students interested in computer vision, infrastructure inspection, vibration measurement, sensing systems, robotics-assisted monitoring, and field-oriented structural health monitoring.

---

## Project Directions

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">

### Drone-Assisted Infrastructure Inspection

Develop drone-based workflows for visual inspection of bridges, buildings, and other civil infrastructure systems.

**Suitable for:** M.Tech / B.Tech / PhD  
**Methods:** drone imaging, computer vision, image processing, inspection planning, AI-assisted damage detection  
**Expected outcomes:** inspection workflows, image datasets, and automated visual assessment tools

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Drone-Based Vibration and Displacement Measurement

Explore the use of drone-mounted or drone-assisted cameras for non-contact measurement of displacement and vibration response.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** video processing, camera stabilization, signal processing, structural dynamics, computer vision  
**Expected outcomes:** feasibility studies, measurement workflows, and validation against conventional sensors

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Multi-View Vision for Structural Monitoring

Develop methods that combine images or videos from multiple viewpoints for improved structural measurement and interpretation.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** multi-view geometry, camera calibration, feature tracking, 3D reconstruction, structural dynamics  
**Expected outcomes:** multi-camera or drone-assisted measurement frameworks for infrastructure monitoring

</div>

</div>

## Useful Background

- Python programming
- Computer vision
- Image and video processing
- Structural dynamics
- Signal processing
- Drone/camera systems
- Machine learning

Students do not need prior drone experience before starting. Interest in sensing, field measurement, and vision-based monitoring is more important.
