System identification focuses on learning mathematical and computational models of engineering systems from measured data. In structural engineering, this is central to understanding dynamic behaviour, detecting changes, and building reliable digital twins.

This theme is suitable for students interested in structural dynamics, nonlinear systems, signal processing, Kalman filtering, sparse regression, and interpretable machine learning.

---

## Project Directions

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">

### Identification of Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

Develop methods to identify interpretable models of nonlinear structural systems from noisy and limited measurements.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** nonlinear dynamics, sparse regression, Kalman filtering, optimization, Python/MATLAB  
**Expected outcomes:** interpretable system identification algorithms and validation on simulated or experimental systems

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Sparse Discovery of Governing Equations

Develop sparse learning approaches to discover governing equations or reduced-order models from measured response data.

**Suitable for:** PhD  
**Methods:** sparse regression, PDE/ODE discovery, scientific machine learning, numerical methods  
**Expected outcomes:** physics-consistent models for engineering systems and publishable computational frameworks

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Kalman Filtering for Structural State Estimation

Develop filtering-based approaches for estimating displacement, velocity, acceleration, and hidden states of structural systems.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** Kalman filtering, Unscented Kalman Filter, data fusion, structural dynamics, signal processing  
**Expected outcomes:** state-estimation workflows for structural monitoring and digital twin applications

</div>

</div>

## Useful Background

- Structural dynamics
- Linear algebra
- Signal processing
- Python/MATLAB programming
- Optimization
- Machine learning
- Kalman filtering

Students do not need expertise in all areas before starting. Interest in mathematical modelling and computation is most important.
