Structural health monitoring focuses on sensing, interpreting, and assessing the condition of civil infrastructure systems such as bridges, buildings, and structural components.

This theme is suitable for students interested in structural dynamics, vibration-based damage detection, sensing, signal processing, machine learning, and infrastructure condition assessment.

---

## Project Directions

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">

### Vibration-Based Damage Detection

Develop methods to detect and localize damage using measured vibration response, modal properties, and data-driven indicators.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** structural dynamics, modal analysis, signal processing, machine learning, uncertainty quantification  
**Expected outcomes:** damage detection algorithms, simulation/experimental validation, publishable SHM studies

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Mechanics-Guided SHM

Develop structural health monitoring methods that combine physics-based understanding with data-driven learning.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** structural dynamics, model updating, physics-guided learning, finite element modelling  
**Expected outcomes:** interpretable SHM frameworks and reliable damage assessment methods

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### Infrastructure Condition Assessment

Develop computational and sensing-based approaches for assessing the condition, performance, and safety of civil infrastructure systems.

**Suitable for:** PhD / M.Tech  
**Methods:** sensing, structural response analysis, data fusion, uncertainty quantification, decision support  
**Expected outcomes:** condition assessment workflows and monitoring strategies for practical infrastructure systems

</div>

</div>

## Useful Background

- Structural dynamics
- Structural analysis
- Signal processing
- Python/MATLAB programming
- Finite element modelling
- Machine learning

Prior expertise in all areas is not required. Students can begin from structural engineering, computation, or sensing backgrounds.
