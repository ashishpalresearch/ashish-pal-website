# About

Prof. Ashish Pal is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.

His research lies at the intersection of structural engineering, scientific computing, sensing technologies, and artificial intelligence. His work focuses on developing mechanics-informed intelligent systems for structural health monitoring, digital twinning, and resilient infrastructure management.

## Research Interests

- Structural Health Monitoring
- Digital Twins of Civil Infrastructure
- Physics-Informed Machine Learning
- Scientific Computing
- Computer Vision-Based Structural Monitoring
- Vibration-Based Damage Detection
- Drone-Based Infrastructure Monitoring
- Uncertainty Quantification
- System Identification of Dynamical Systems

## Research Vision

The long-term goal of the research is to develop intelligent infrastructure systems capable of autonomous sensing, interpretation, prediction, and decision support by integrating mechanics, data, and machine intelligence.
